# log_009

Firmware
--------
4.6.3

Summary
-------
Two landing approaches.

Approach 1
----------

Outcome
-------
Go-around.

Notes
-----
Flare commanded too high.
Aircraft slowed towards stall.
Pilot selected FBWA and completed the landing manually.

Approach 2
----------

Outcome
-------
Successful landing.

Notes
-----
Takeoff performed while ArduPlane remained in the LAND sequence.
AUTO re-engaged after takeoff.
Flare again initiated too high.
Pilot used stick mixing to lower the nose and fly the aircraft onto the runway.

Observations
------------
- LAND window contains two separate approaches.
- Useful regression log for interrupted landing sequences.
- Demonstrates high-flare behaviour.
- Good test case for LandingWindows.
- Good test case for RFND and future BARO/ARSPD detectors.
